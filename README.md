# Take One

A late-night video shop that picks your film for you.

Upload your Letterboxd export, and the shop stocks its shelves from your watchlist and everything you've seen. Roll for one film or three, filter by mood, length, subtitles and era, and go in blind: no scores are ever shown before you watch.

Film data and posters from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Not affiliated with Letterboxd.

Everything you upload stays on your own device.
